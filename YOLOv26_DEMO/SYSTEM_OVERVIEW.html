Where $\hat{y} \in [0,1]$ is the normalized vertical coordinate, $W$ & $H$ are frame dimensions.

4. Spatial Fusion ??Distance & Threat Estimation

4.1 Monocular Distance Estimation

$$ \text{distance} = \frac{W_{\text{class}} \times f}{\text{bbox\_width}} - 3\text{m} $$

where $W_{\text{class}}$ is the per-class real width and $f$ is the camera focal length (800 px). The $-3\text{m}$ offset corrects for the distance from camera to object center ??object rear edge.

4.2 Relative Speed (Kalman EMA Tracking)

Pure vision ??no external sensors required.

$$ v_t = \alpha \cdot \frac{d_{t-1} - d_t}{\Delta t} + (1-\alpha) \cdot v_{t-1} $$

$\alpha = 0.4$. Objects matched across frames by bounding-box center distance (< 80 px threshold). Fallback $v_0 = 15$ m/s (54 km/h) for unmatched objects.
